<h2>FAQ</h2>
<p><strong>Should mountain captions be short or long?</strong> Both work for different purposes. Short captions are memorable and punchy. Longer captions that share your story or reflection can be more engaging. Authenticity matters more than length.</p>
<p><strong>What makes a good mountain Instagram caption?</strong> A good caption matches the photo&#039;s mood—whether awe-inspiring, funny, or reflective. It should feel authentic and enhance the story behind the image.</p>
<p><strong>Can I use funny mountain captions?</strong> Absolutely. Humorous captions about sore legs, snack breaks, or getting lost are relatable and engaging. Not every mountain post needs to be deep and philosophical.</p>
